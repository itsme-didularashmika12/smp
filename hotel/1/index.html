Your Family's Home Away from Home

Located just steps away from the historical Sri Maha Bodhi, Rajarata Family Resort offers a tranquil escape for those seeking both spiritual connection and modern comfort. Our resort is designed with families in mind, featuring lush tropical gardens, spacious interiors, and a peaceful atmosphere.

Whether you're exploring the ancient ruins of Jetavanaramaya or enjoying a quiet evening in our garden, we provide the perfect base for your Anuradhapura pilgrimage.
